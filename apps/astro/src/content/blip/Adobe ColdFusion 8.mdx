## Context

Adobe ColdFusion 8 remains present in legacy workloads, but it sits well outside the current engineering baseline. The team needs a clear record that it is tolerated only for maintenance and migration purposes, not for new delivery.

## Decision

Mark Adobe ColdFusion 8 as deprecated. Keep it running only where necessary to support existing business capability while actively preferring migration paths toward more maintainable platforms.

## Consequences

- Creates a clear signal that new investment should not target this runtime.
- Supports prioritisation of migration work for affected systems.
- Leaves ongoing maintenance cost in place until replacement work is completed.
