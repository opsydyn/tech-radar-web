## Context

Axon Framework was assessed to support event-driven workflows in domains where explicit command and query separation improves clarity. The team wanted a toolset that could model message handling and event publication consistently, while still being selective about where that complexity is justified.

## Decision

Adopt Axon Framework only for bounded contexts that genuinely benefit from CQRS and event-driven coordination. For simpler CRUD-oriented services, stay with lighter architectural patterns and avoid introducing Axon by default.

## Consequences

- Gives teams a structured approach to commands, events, and domain messaging.
- Improves consistency in places where event-driven flow is central to the domain model.
- Adds operational and conceptual overhead, so adoption should remain deliberate rather than default.
