# Tech Radar Edition 1

## Introduction

Welcome to the first edition of our Technology Radar. This edition highlights the key technologies, tools, techniques, and platforms that we believe are worth adopting, trialing, assessing, or holding for now.

## Key Highlights

### Platforms
- **Kubernetes** continues to be the dominant container orchestration platform
- **AWS Lambda** and serverless architectures are gaining more adoption
- **Edge Computing** is becoming more mainstream

### Languages & Frameworks
- **Rust** is seeing increased adoption for performance-critical applications
- **React** remains a strong choice for frontend development
- **Next.js** is our recommended React framework

### Tools
- **GitHub Actions** has become our standard CI/CD solution
- **Docker** remains essential for containerization
- **Terraform** is our preferred IaC tool

### Techniques
- **Micro-frontends** are being adopted for large-scale applications
- **API-first design** is becoming standard practice
- **Feature flags** are recommended for all new features

## New Entries

The following technologies have been added to our radar in this edition:

- **Apache Beam** - Trial
- **WebAssembly** - Assess
- **Deno** - Assess
- **Svelte** - Trial

## Moved Out

The following technologies have been moved out of our radar:

- **Angular.js** (not Angular)
- **Jenkins** (replaced by GitHub Actions)
- **Puppet** (replaced by Terraform)

## Conclusion

This edition of the Tech Radar reflects our current thinking and recommendations. We encourage teams to consider these technologies when making architectural decisions.
