# Tech Radar Edition 4 - December 2024

## Introduction

Our year-end 2024 edition captures a full year of technology evolution and adoption patterns.

## Key Movements

### Technologies Showing Caution
- **JWT** - Concerns emerging about token management in large systems
- Re-evaluating authentication approaches for scale

### Strong Adoption
- **Effect-TS** - Now in Adopt after successful production usage
- **XState** - Proven for complex state management
- **Vanilla Extract** - Standard for CSS-in-JS

### New Considerations
- **OAuth2** - Assessing as JWT alternative
- **PASETO** - Evaluating as token format

## Architectural Shifts

- Moving towards event-driven patterns
- Increased focus on observability
- Domain-driven design principles gaining traction

## Conclusion

2024 showed significant maturation in our functional programming adoption while revealing scale challenges with some earlier choices.
