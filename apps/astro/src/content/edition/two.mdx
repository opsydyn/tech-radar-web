# Tech Radar Edition 2 - December 2023

## Introduction

Our second edition captures the state of our technology landscape at the end of 2023. This edition shows the evolution of technologies tracked in our first edition, plus new additions.

## Key Movements

### Technologies Growing
- **JWT** - Moved from Trial to Adopt after successful adoption across services
- **React** - Continued growth in usage
- **TypeScript** - Now standard across all frontend projects

### New Entries
- **XState** - Added to Trial for complex state management
- **Effect-TS** - Assessing for functional programming patterns

### Technologies on Hold
- **Redux** - Prefer lighter state management solutions

## Highlights

### Techniques
- **JWT authentication** has proven reliable and scalable across our microservices
- API-first design continues to show benefits
- Event-driven architecture gaining traction

## Conclusion

This edition reflects our continued evolution towards more functional, type-safe approaches while maintaining pragmatic choices for production systems.
