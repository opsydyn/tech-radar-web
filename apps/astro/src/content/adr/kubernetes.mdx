<h1 class:list={[styles.twStyle]}>Kubernetes</h1>

## Context

As our application scales, we need a more robust orchestration solution for our containerized applications. Manual management of containers is becoming increasingly complex and time-consuming.

## Decision

We are evaluating Kubernetes as our container orchestration platform. Kubernetes provides powerful features for automating deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications.

## Consequences

- Improved scalability and reliability of our containerized applications
- Automated handling of container failures and scaling
- Significant learning curve for the operations team
- Need for additional infrastructure to support a Kubernetes cluster
