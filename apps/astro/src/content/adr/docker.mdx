## Context

Our deployment process has become increasingly complex, with inconsistencies between development and production environments causing issues. We need a solution that provides consistent environments across development, testing, and production.

## Decision

We have decided to adopt Docker for containerization of our applications. Docker's ability to package applications with their dependencies into standardized containers will help us achieve consistency across environments.

## Consequences

- Improved environment consistency across development, testing, and production
- Simplified onboarding process for new developers
- Need for additional training on Docker concepts and best practices
- Potential for increased complexity in our CI/CD pipeline initially
