## Context

Our application needs a database solution that can handle flexible schema requirements and scale horizontally as our user base grows. We're evaluating NoSQL options to complement our existing relational database.

## Decision

After careful evaluation, we have decided NOT to adopt MongoDB for our primary data storage. While MongoDB offers benefits in terms of schema flexibility and horizontal scaling, our analysis showed that our data has strong relational characteristics that are better served by our existing PostgreSQL database.

## Consequences

- We will continue using PostgreSQL with JSON/JSONB columns for flexible schema needs
- We avoid the operational complexity of managing multiple database systems
- We maintain ACID compliance for our critical data
- We'll leverage PostgreSQL's scaling capabilities for our current growth projections
